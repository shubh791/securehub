SecureHub policies

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information when you explore or take part in SecureHub learning services.

Effective date: 5 August 2026. Please read this policy alongside our Terms of Service.

Information we may collect

When you enquire, request a callback, attend an event, subscribe to updates or enrol in a programme, we may collect your name, contact details, city, education or work background, learning interests and enquiry history. We only ask for information that helps us respond to you, support your learning or administer your enrolment.

How information is collected

We receive information directly from forms, calls, email, classroom interactions and learner-support conversations. Basic technical information such as browser type, device information and pages visited may also be collected to help our website work reliably and improve the learner experience.

How we use information

SecureHub uses information to provide course guidance, confirm admissions, arrange sessions, share requested programme details, deliver learner support and improve our services. With your permission where required, we may send relevant programme, batch or event updates. You can opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time.

When information may be shared

Access to personal information is limited to authorised SecureHub team members and carefully selected service providers who support operations such as communications, payments or learning delivery. We do not sell personal information for unrelated marketing. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect learners, our services or our rights.

Storage and protection

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No online system is completely secure, so we encourage you not to send passwords or sensitive payment details by ordinary email. Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or to meet legal and operational obligations.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to review, correct or remove personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may also withdraw consent for optional communications. To make a request, email admissions@securehub.in with enough information for us to identify your enquiry safely.

Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will always appear on this page. Continued use of our services after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.
