SecureHub policies

Privacy Policy

How SecureHub may collect, use and protect information when you enquire about or take part in our learning services.

Information we may collect

When you contact SecureHub, request a callback, subscribe to updates or enrol in a programme, we may collect contact details and information needed to respond to your request.

How we use information

Information may be used to provide course guidance, share requested programme details, improve learner support and send relevant updates where you have chosen to receive them.

Sharing and protection

SecureHub limits access to personal information to authorised team members and service providers that support its learning operations. Information is not sold for unrelated marketing.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to review, correct or remove your information, or opt out of non-essential updates. Contact admissions@securehub.in for requests.
