SecureHub policies

Refund Policy

A clear policy for handling programme cancellation, transfers and refund requests fairly.

Submitting a request

Refund or cancellation requests should be submitted in writing with the learner name, enrolled programme and payment reference. SecureHub should acknowledge the request and explain the next step.

Eligibility

Refund eligibility, deductions and transfer options depend on the programme, start date, provided learning materials and attendance. Exact timelines and conditions should be confirmed before enrolment.

Processing refunds

Where a refund is approved, SecureHub should process it through the original payment method where practical and confirm the expected completion time in writing.

Contact us

For a refund-policy query, contact admissions@securehub.in or call +91 98765 43210.
