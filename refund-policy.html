Refund Policy

A clear process for programme cancellation, transfers and refund requests, with details confirmed before enrolment.

Effective date: 5 August 2026. Your programme’s admission confirmation may include additional terms.

Submitting a request

To request a cancellation, transfer or refund, email admissions@securehub.in with the learner name, enrolled programme, registered contact number, payment reference and reason for the request. Written requests help us review the right enrolment record and provide a clear response.

How requests are reviewed

We review the programme selected, batch start date, attendance, materials or platform access already provided, and the terms accepted at enrolment. A member of our team will acknowledge the request and explain whether more information is needed. Submitting a request does not by itself confirm eligibility for a refund.

Refund eligibility

Eligibility, any deductions and available transfer options depend on the individual programme and the point at which the request is received. Programme-specific fee schedules and conditions communicated before enrolment take priority. Fees for completed learning, delivered materials, third-party examination bookings or services already used may not be refundable where this has been disclosed.

Batch transfers and alternatives

Where suitable, we may offer a transfer to a later batch, another available programme or a learner-support solution instead of cancellation. Transfers are subject to seat availability, programme suitability and any difference in fees. We will explain the available option and any financial impact before making a change.

Approved refund processing

If a refund is approved, SecureHub will confirm the approved amount and processing timeline in writing. Refunds are normally returned through the original payment method where practical. Processing time can vary depending on the payment provider and banking system; we will share an update if a delay needs attention.

Need help before you enrol?

Please ask for the programme-specific cancellation and payment terms before completing your enrolment. For refund-policy questions, contact admissions@securehub.in or call +91 98765 43210. Keeping the conversation in writing helps both you and our team track the request accurately.
