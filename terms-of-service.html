Our learning services

SecureHub provides instructor-led training, practical learning support, career guidance and related educational services as described for each programme. Course schedules, delivery formats, module coverage, tools and certification preparation can vary by programme. Please confirm the details that apply to your chosen batch before you enrol.

Enrolment is confirmed only after the required admission and payment steps are completed. You are responsible for providing accurate contact and enrolment information and for meeting any stated prerequisites. Where a programme has particular device, software, attendance or eligibility requirements, these will be communicated before the batch begins.

Programme fees, payment schedules, taxes and any applicable charges will be communicated during the admission process. Unless stated otherwise in writing, payment does not guarantee a particular job, salary, certification result or interview outcome. Learners should use classroom sessions, labs, course materials and community spaces responsibly and respectfully. SecureHub may take reasonable steps to protect learners, instructors, systems and the learning environment.

Course materials and intellectual property

Course notes, recordings, assignments, lab material and brand assets are provided for your personal learning use unless we agree otherwise in writing. You may not reproduce, distribute, sell or make them publicly available without permission. Your own project work remains yours, while SecureHub may display anonymised learner outcomes or feedback only with appropriate care and consent where required.

Changes, availability and outcomes

We may reasonably update schedules, instructors, learning tools or module order to maintain programme quality. We will aim to communicate material changes promptly. Career support is designed to strengthen your readiness; hiring decisions, offer terms and employment outcomes remain with the relevant employer and learner.

Questions and contact
